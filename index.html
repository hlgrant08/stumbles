who are we?

We are the Stumbles. We are a 5-piece rock band based in Glasgow and perform some banging covers whilst making our own stuff. Stumbles was formed in 2023 by Zak Nicol and Harry Grant, our band now consists of the following lineup:

  • Lead Vocalist: Reuben Grant
  • Lead Guitar: Finlay Hughes
  • Rhythm Guitar: Felix Gallagher
  • Bass Guitar: Zak Nicol
  • Drums: Harry Grant

Most of our original music is written by our guitarist, Finlay, who does the main melody and rhythm, while Reuben does our lyrics. We all create our own parts for every song we make and put genuine effort into creating the stuff we think sounds great. That's what music is all about.

who are we?

We are the Stumbles: a five-piece rock band based in Glasgow, Scotland, performing a mix of original music and covers. Formed in 2023 by Zak Nicol and Harry Grant, we bring together five musicians with different influences, styles and ideas to create our own distinctive sound.


band members

Lead Vocalist: Reuben Grant
Lead Guitar: Finlay Hughes
Rhythm Guitar: Felix Gallagher
Bass Guitar: Zak Nicol
Drums: Harry Grant


our music

Our original music is primarily driven by Finlay, who creates melodic guitar lines and rhythmic instrumental parts, while Reuben writes the lyrics. Each member brings their own style, technique and creative ideas to the table, allowing us to develop our sound through collaboration, experimentation and a shared passion for music.


For us, it’s about more than just playing and writing. It’s about bringing different perspectives and ideas together to create something of our own. That’s what our music is all about.
